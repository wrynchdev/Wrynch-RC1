How it works

Shoot the stage. Confirm. Send.

01

Shoot the whole stage

One burst for under the hood, one for under the car. Any order. No tapping into each point first.

02

AI reads every photo

It finds each part in the shot and notes its condition: looks OK, or what's wrong. One photo of a wheel can cover the tire, pad, rotor and caliper at once.

03

The tech confirms

Accept, change or reject each suggestion and enter the measurements. Nothing counts until the tech confirms it.

04

The customer gets it

A phone report in plain words the tech approved, with photos and last visit's numbers. Customers approve work right from the report.

AI you control

AI suggests. Your tech decides.

  • Every AI suggestion stays pending until a tech confirms or changes it, including “looks OK.”
  • Nothing unconfirmed reaches the customer: not a finding, a photo or a sentence. There's no setting to turn that off.
  • AI can reword a note for the customer, but can't add a finding, a measurement or a repair.
  • Measurements come from the tech's gauge, never from a photo guess.

Questions shops ask

Do my techs have to change how they inspect?

No. They keep your points and shoot each stage in one go. The part-level detail comes from how photos and findings are sorted, not from extra taps.

Can one photo count for several parts?

Yes. A shot of a front wheel can show the tire, pad, rotor and caliper. The AI notes the condition of each part it can see, and the photo appears under every inspection point it covers. The tech can add or remove parts from any photo.

What if the AI gets it wrong?

The tech changes the parts on the photo or rejects the finding. Until they confirm, nothing counts and the customer can't see it.

Can I use my own limits?

Yes. Every threshold is yours to set, in mm or 32nds. Past results keep the rules they were rated with.
