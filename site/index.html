How it works

From the bay to the customer’s phone.

Your tech shoots, the AI sorts, your tech signs off, and your customer approves the work.

01

Shoot each stage

Road test, under the hood, under the car. One burst of photos per stage, in any order. No tapping into each point first.

02

AI reads every photo

It matches each photo to the parts behind that stage's points and notes what it sees: looks OK, or what's wrong. One shot of a front wheel can cover the tire, pad, rotor and caliper.

03

Your tech signs off

Confirm, change or reject each suggestion, and enter measurements from the gauge. Nothing counts until the tech says so.

04

The customer approves

Your advisor adds prices and sends the report by text, email or link. The customer sees photos, plain-language notes and last visit's numbers, and approves work from their phone.

AI you control

AI does the sorting. Your tech makes the call.

  • Every suggestion, including “looks OK,” waits for a tech to confirm it.
  • Nothing unconfirmed reaches the customer: no finding, no photo, no sentence. There’s no setting to turn that off.
  • When a photo can’t show which side of the car a part is on, the AI says so and your tech picks.
  • AI can reword a note for the customer, but it never adds a finding, a measurement or a repair. Measurements come from the tech’s gauge.

Questions shops ask

Do my techs have to change how they inspect?

No. They keep your points and shoot each stage in one go. The part-by-part detail comes from how the photos are sorted, not from extra taps.

Can one photo count for several parts?

Yes. A shot of a front wheel can show the tire, pad, rotor and caliper. The AI notes each part it can see from that stage, and the photo shows up under every point it covers. Your tech can add or remove parts on any photo.

What if the AI gets it wrong?

Your tech changes the parts on the photo or rejects the finding. Until they confirm, nothing counts and the customer can’t see it.

Can I set my own limits?

Yes, every threshold, in mm or 32nds. Past inspections keep the rules they were rated under.

How do customers get the report?

By text, email or a link you copy. It opens on their phone with no app to install and no login, and they can approve work right there.
